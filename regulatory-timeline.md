# Regulatory Reporting Timeline

## Overview

This document outlines the regulatory reporting obligations for adverse event reports associated with Cyperidone across the three fictional markets where it is registered: Australia (TGA), European Union (EMA), and United States (FDA). Timelines and requirements are based on real regulatory frameworks applied to this fictional drug for educational purposes.

## Reporting Timeline Summary

### Expedited Reports (Serious Adverse Events)

| Region | Regulatory Body | Report Type | Timeline | Applicable Cases |
|--------|----------------|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Australia | TGA | ICSR via TGA Business Services | 15 calendar days from MAH awareness | All serious AEs, regardless of listedness |
| EU | EMA (via EudraVigilance) | Expedited ICSR | 15 calendar days from MAH awareness for serious AEs occurring in or outside the EEA. Non-serious AEs from outside the EEA are not submitted individually but included in aggregate PSUR/PBRER data | Case 001 (serious, expedited); Case 002 excluded from individual EudraVigilance submission (non-serious, non-EEA) |
| USA | FDA (via FAERS) | 15-Day Alert Report (MedWatch 3500A) | 15 calendar days for serious and unexpected; 15 calendar days for all serious AEs that are both serious and related | Case 001 if unlisted in US PI |

### Non-Expedited Reports (Non-Serious Adverse Events)

| Region | Regulatory Body | Report Type | Timeline | Applicable Cases |
|--------|----------------|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Australia | TGA | Include in Periodic Safety Update Report (PSUR) | Per agreed PSUR schedule (typically 6-monthly for first 2 years post-approval) | Case 002 (non-serious, listed) |
| EU | EMA | Include in PSUR/PBRER (Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Report) | Per agreed PBRER schedule | Case 002 |
| USA | FDA | Include in Periodic Adverse Drug Experience Report (PADER) | Quarterly for first 3 years, annually thereafter | Case 002 |

## Reporting Workflow: Case 001 (Serious Agranulocytosis)

### Day 0: Report Receipt (14 April 2025)

- Spontaneous report received from treating psychiatrist via telephone
- Initial triage: serious (life-threatening, hospitalisation required)
- Case entered into MAH internal safety database
- Clock starts for regulatory reporting timeline

### Day 0 to Day 3: Initial Processing

- Case data entered and quality checked in safety database
- MedDRA coding applied (PT: Agranulocytosis, SOC: Blood and lymphatic system disorders)
- Seriousness criteria confirmed: life-threatening and hospitalisation
- Listedness/expectedness assessed against approved Product Information
- Causality assessment performed using WHO-UMC system: Probable/Likely
- Medical review by qualified pharmacovigilance physician completed

### Day 3 to Day 10: Report Preparation

- ICSR form completed in E2B(R3) format for electronic submission
- Clinical narrative drafted and medically reviewed
- Supporting laboratory data attached
- Quality review of completed ICSR by second PV professional

### Day 10 to Day 15: Submission

- ICSR submitted electronically to TGA via TGA Business Services portal
- ICSR submitted to EMA via EudraVigilance
- ICSR submitted to FDA via FAERS (if applicable under US reporting criteria)
- Submission confirmations archived in case file

### Post-Submission: Follow-Up

- Follow-up information requested from reporter (discharge summary, final haematology)
- Follow-up ICSR submitted within 15 calendar days of receiving significant new information
- Case reviewed for signal detection implications
- Case flagged for inclusion in next PSUR/PBRER

## Reporting Workflow: Case 002 (Non-Serious QTc Prolongation)

### Day 0: Report Receipt (28 April 2025)

- Written report received from cardiologist via email
- Initial triage: non-serious (no hospitalisation, no life-threatening criteria met)
- Case entered into MAH internal safety database

### Day 0 to Day 5: Processing

- Case data entered and quality checked
- MedDRA coding applied (PT: Electrocardiogram QT prolonged)
- Listedness confirmed: QTc prolongation is listed at common frequency
- Causality assessment: Possible (confounded by family cardiac history)
- Medical review completed

### Non-Expedited Pathway

- Case does not require expedited (15-day) reporting to any regulatory authority
- Case filed for inclusion in next PSUR/PBRER cycle
- Case included in aggregate cardiac safety data for ongoing signal monitoring
