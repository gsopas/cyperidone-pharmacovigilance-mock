## Clinical Narrative

A 38-year-old female patient with a history of treatment-resistant schizophrenia was commenced on Cyperidone 4 mg once daily on 06 January 2025 after inadequate response to sequential trials of olanzapine, quetiapine, and aripiprazole. Prior to initiation, baseline haematology was within normal limits: white blood cell count (WBC) 6.2 x 10^9/L and absolute neutrophil count (ANC) 3.8 x 10^9/L.

Routine blood monitoring at Week 8 (03 March 2025) showed WBC 4.1 x 10^9/L and ANC 2.1 x 10^9/L, representing a decline from baseline but remaining within the normal reference range. The treating psychiatrist continued treatment with increased monitoring frequency to fortnightly.

On 12 April 2025 (Day 96 of treatment), the patient presented to the emergency department with fever (38.9 degrees Celsius), sore throat, and general malaise. Urgent blood work revealed WBC 1.1 x 10^9/L and ANC 0.3 x 10^9/L, consistent with agranulocytosis (ANC < 0.5 x 10^9/L).

Cyperidone was immediately discontinued on 15 April 2025. The patient was admitted to hospital and commenced on prophylactic broad-spectrum antibiotics (piperacillin-tazobactam) and granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF) to support neutrophil recovery. Blood cultures were drawn and returned negative at 48 hours.

Follow-up blood work on 22 April 2025 showed ANC recovering to 1.4 x 10^9/L. The patient remained hospitalised for monitoring. Positive dechallenge was established as the ANC began recovering following drug discontinuation.

No rechallenge was attempted. The treating psychiatrist has been advised to consider clozapine with full haematological monitoring as an alternative, given the patient's treatment-resistant profile.
