# Signal Detection Summary

## Cyperidone Post-Marketing Safety Signal Analysis (Fictional)

### Reporting Period: March 2024 to April 2025 (13 months post-launch)

## Background

Cyperidone received TGA registration in March 2024 for the treatment of schizophrenia. An estimated 4,200 patients have been exposed to Cyperidone in Australia during the reporting period based on dispensing data. The global exposure estimate across all approved markets is approximately 38,000 patient-years.

This signal detection summary reviews aggregate spontaneous adverse event reports received during the first 13 months of post-marketing surveillance.

## Signals Under Evaluation

### Signal 1: Haematological Toxicity (Agranulocytosis / Severe Neutropenia)

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Signal Source | Spontaneous ICSR reports |
| Number of Cases | 5 cases of severe neutropenia (ANC < 1.0), including 2 cases of agranulocytosis (ANC < 0.5) |
| Reporting Rate | 1.19 per 1,000 exposed patients (cumulative incidence proportion based on 4,200 AU patient exposures over 13 months) |
| Expected Rate (class background) | 0.1 to 0.3 per 1,000 patient-years for non-clozapine atypical antipsychotics |
| Disproportionality Signal | Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR) = 3.2 compared against all other non-clozapine atypical antipsychotics within the TGA adverse event reporting database (threshold for signal: PRR > 2.0) |
| Signal Status | Under active evaluation |

**Assessment:** The observed reporting rate for severe neutropenia and agranulocytosis exceeds the expected class background rate and the PRR exceeds the signal detection threshold. While spontaneous reporting data is subject to stimulated reporting bias (particularly early post-launch), the signal warrants further investigation.

**Recommended Actions:**
1. Conduct a cumulative review of all haematological AE cases with detailed clinical analysis
2. Evaluate whether a causal mechanism can be hypothesised based on Cyperidone's structural modifications (cyano group electronic effects on bone marrow progenitor cells)
3. Consider whether product information requires strengthened haematological monitoring recommendations
4. Prepare a signal assessment report for the next PSUR/PBRER

### Signal 2: QTc Prolongation in Patients with Cardiac Risk Factors

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Signal Source | Spontaneous ICSR reports and post-marketing observational data |
| Number of Cases | 31 cardiac disorder reports, of which 14 specifically documented QTc prolongation |
| Cases with QTc > 480 ms | 6 cases |
| Cases with QTc > 500 ms | 1 case (concurrent QTc-prolonging concomitant medication) |
| Proportion with cardiac risk factors | 9 of 14 QTc cases (64%) had pre-existing cardiac risk factors |
| Signal Status | Monitored, no new action required at this time |

**Assessment:** QTc prolongation is a listed and expected adverse reaction for Cyperidone. The majority of cases with clinically significant QTc values occurred in patients with pre-existing cardiac risk factors. The single case exceeding 500 ms involved concomitant use of a known QTc-prolonging medication. The current product information includes appropriate cardiac monitoring recommendations. No signal of unexpected QTc risk beyond the known class effect is identified at this time.

**Recommended Actions:**
1. Continue routine monitoring of cardiac AE reports
2. Assess whether product information should more explicitly address patients with pre-existing cardiac risk factors
3. Include aggregate cardiac data in the next PSUR/PBRER

### Signal 3: Hepatic Transaminase Elevations

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Signal Source | Spontaneous ICSR reports |
| Number of Cases | 7 hepatobiliary disorder reports, of which 4 documented transaminase elevations > 3x ULN |
| Serious Cases | 2 (one case with ALT > 10x ULN requiring hospitalisation) |
| Signal Status | Under monitoring |

**Assessment:** Hepatic transaminase elevations are an uncommon but listed adverse reaction for atypical antipsychotics. The number of reports is small and consistent with background class rates. The single case with ALT > 10x ULN is currently under individual case review. No disproportionality signal is detected at this stage.

**Recommended Actions:**
1. Complete individual case review of the serious hepatic case
2. Continue routine monitoring
3. No label change recommended at this time

## Conclusions

The aggregate safety data for Cyperidone during the first 13 months of post-marketing surveillance is broadly consistent with the known safety profile of the atypical antipsychotic class. One signal requiring active investigation has been identified: a potentially elevated rate of severe neutropenia and agranulocytosis compared to the non-clozapine atypical antipsychotic class background rate. This signal will be the subject of a formal signal assessment report and will be discussed in the next PSUR/PBRER submission.
